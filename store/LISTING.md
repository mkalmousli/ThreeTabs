## Description (plain text, paste as-is)
Too many tabs = too little focus. ThreeTabs gives your browser a hard limit: only 3 tabs open at a time (you choose the number, 1 to 50). Try to open another one and it never gets to exist.

No willpower needed. No dashboards, no guilt. Just a limit that keeps you on the one thing in front of you.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick your limit. Three by default, adjustable from 1 to 50.
• Open one tab too many (Ctrl+T, a link, a new window) and it is closed instantly.
• Finish something, close a tab, then open the next one. One thing at a time.

FEATURES
• Hard tab limit: 3 by default, 1 to 50 in settings
• Blocks new tabs and new windows once you are at the limit
• Optional "redirect" mode: a blocked link opens in the tab you came from instead of being lost
• Count across all windows, or give every window its own budget
• Quiet by design: no numbers or badges nagging you in the toolbar
• Popup with your three slots, a focus-mode switch and a blocked-tabs counter
• Never closes tabs you already have open. Only new ones are stopped.
• Focus mode toggle for the moments you really need more tabs
• Settings always open, even when you are over the limit

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts
• Never reads or sends your browsing history
• Settings stay on your device
• The only network request is an optional once-a-day check for a new version on GitHub (can be turned off in settings)

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
