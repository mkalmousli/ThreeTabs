Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Applies to ThreeTabs 1.1.0 and later

In short: ThreeTabs has no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no server of its own. It never reads or sends your browsing history. Your settings stay on your device. The one network request it makes is an optional daily check of GitHub for a new version, which you can turn off.

1. Who we are

ThreeTabs is a free and open-source browser extension by Mohamad Almousli (mkalmousli, the "developer"). The source code is public at github.com/mkalmousli/ThreeTabs, so every claim here can be checked in the code.

2. Data the extension stores (locally only)

The extension saves the following in your browser's extension storage (storage.local). It never leaves your device and the developer cannot see it. If your browser syncs extension data, that is done by the browser, not by ThreeTabs.

DataPurpose
Tab limit, count scope (all windows / per window), block mode, on/off, update-check preferenceYour settings
Number of tabs blocked (total, and today)Shown in the popup. Just two numbers, with no record of which sites
Latest known version numberTo show "update available"
A short-lived timestampTo allow the settings page to open when you click "Settings"

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

3. Permissions and why

  • tabs: to count open tabs and close a new tab when you are over the limit. The extension reads a new tab's address only to decide whether to redirect it (the "open in current tab" mode) and to recognise its own settings page. Addresses are used in memory at that moment and are never stored, logged or transmitted.
  • storage: to save the settings above.
  • alarms: to schedule the daily update check.

4. Network requests

The extension makes one kind of request: once a day it fetches https://api.github.com/repos/mkalmousli/ThreeTabs/releases/latest to see whether a newer version exists. The request carries no identifier and no personal data, but GitHub, like any web server, receives your IP address and standard request headers and handles them under GitHub's privacy statement. You can turn it off in Settings → Check GitHub for new versions. With it off, the extension makes no network requests at all.

5. What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, fingerprinting or advertising.
  • No collection or sale of browsing history, tab titles, page content or personal data.
  • No third-party code or remotely loaded scripts.

6. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We run no analytics or cookies on it. GitHub, as the host, may process visitor IP addresses and keep server logs under its own policy. The promo video is embedded from YouTube (privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com mode), but it only loads after you click play. Until then, no request is made to YouTube; once loaded, YouTube handles your data under Google's privacy policy. The same video is also available as a direct MP4 download from this site. The site links to external services (GitHub, Ko-fi) that have their own privacy practices. If you click the Ko-fi link, any donation is handled entirely by Ko-fi. The developer never sees your payment details.

7. Children

ThreeTabs collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will be published here and in the repository's history. A change that adds any data collection would require a new, clearly announced version.

9. Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/mkalmousli/ThreeTabs/issues.
